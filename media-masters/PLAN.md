## 1. The one-page version

**The target.** D3 Corp (d3corp.com) is a 30-year-old, 40-person, full-service digital agency
with offices in West Ocean City and Salisbury, MD. They sell websites, SEO (including AI search
optimization), paid ads, social, email, graphic design and branding, print, and video/photo/drone.
They have case studies with marquee local names (Jolly Roger, Boardwalk Hotel Group, Farmers Bank
of Willards, Visiting Angels, Salisbury Chamber), a 5.0 rating on at least one directory, and a
domain that has been earning links since the 1990s.

**Us.** Media Masters Group LLC has no findable website, Google Business Profile, directory
listing, or social profile today. We start from zero. That is not a problem, but it dictates the
order of work: launch fast, start the slow-compounding local signals (reviews, citations, Google
Business Profile) on day one, and use paid search as a bridge while organic authority builds.

**The strategy in one sentence.** Match every service D3 sells, out-execute them on the things
buyers actually compare (speed of response, price transparency, proof, the quality of our own
site), and become the most complete, fastest, best-reviewed, most-cited agency across Google,
Bing, Apple Maps, and the AI assistants people now ask for recommendations.

**Four phases.**

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Foundations | Week 0–1 | Intake facts, domain, Google Business Profile, accounts, trademark check, unblock the agents' network access |
| 1 Agent analysis | Week 1–2 | Five Opus agents dissect d3corp.com and the local market, then produce the site blueprint |
| 2 Build the better site | Weeks 2–6 | Everything D3 does, plus what they don't, on a faster and more convincing site |
| 3 Search dominance | Week 4 onward | Technical, local, content, authority, AI-search, and paid; ongoing |
| 4 Measure and iterate | Monthly | Rank tracking against D3, review velocity, leads; monthly agent sprint |

**What "beat them" means, in numbers (targets, not guarantees).**

| Measure | 90 days | 180 days | 365 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site | Live, 30+ pages indexed, Core Web Vitals green | 60+ pages, 8+ case studies | 100+ pages, 15+ case studies |
| Google reviews | 25+ | 60+ | 120+ (D3 has roughly 35–46 on directories) |
| Google local pack (map 3-pack) | Top 3 for 3 core Ocean City terms | Top 3 for 10 terms incl. Salisbury | Top 3 across all target towns |
| Organic rankings | Page 1 for 5 long-tail terms | Page 1 for 15; ahead of D3 on 5 of 20 tracked terms | Ahead of D3 on 12+ of 20 tracked terms |
| Inbound leads from search + paid | 5+/month | 12+/month | 25+/month |

Plain truth about timing: a 30-year-old domain cannot be out-ranked on head terms in 90 days.
The local map pack and long-tail terms can be won in 3–6 months. Head terms such as
"web design Ocean City MD" and "marketing agency Ocean City MD" take 6–18 months of
consistent work. Paid search gives us page-one presence from week 3 while that happens.

## 2. What we know about D3 Corp today

Full notes with sources are in `research/00-d3-recon-2026-10-13.md`. The short version:

**Strengths we must respect**
- 30 years of brand, a link profile to match, and two physical offices (both earn map-pack rankings).
- A complete service menu with a dedicated page for each service.
- Real local proof: named case studies in hospitality, banking, tourism, home services, and healthcare.
- Already pitching "AI and generative engine optimization," so they are not asleep on AI search.
- Semrush Agency Partner and WP Engine Agency Directory badges.

**Openings we found (to be verified by the Phase 1 agents)**
1. **No public pricing.** Their terms even say SEO and PPC are extra charges "if requested." Price transparency is wide open.
2. **PR is promised on the homepage but has no service page.**
3. **The blog looks thin and dated** for a 40-person agency.
4. **Their name and address are inconsistent across directories** (D3, D3Corp, D3 Corp, D-3 Corporation). Sloppy citations hurt local rankings.
5. **Review volume is modest for 30 years in business**, and Yelp holds only 3 reviews.
6. **Employer reviews are middling** (3.0 and 3.4), which often means turnover. "You always work with the owner" is a credible counter-position.
7. **They only claim Ocean City and Salisbury.** No pages for Berlin, Ocean Pines, the Delaware beaches, or Sussex County, DE.
8. **Typical project budget under $10,000** (per WP Engine's listing). There is room both above and below that.

**Other local players** (none dominant): Standout Fast (Salisbury), Full City Media, Sure Sites,
Ocean Computer Tech, Studio For Publications (Ocean City), plus out-of-area firms running Ocean
City and Salisbury landing pages (Prager Microsystems, EZ Marketing). MGH in Baltimore holds the
Town of Ocean City's own tourism account. One directory counts only two web design companies in
Ocean City. The pool is thin, which is good for us.

## 3. Phase 0 — Foundations (week 0–1, before the agents run)

Everything here is a prerequisite. Most items are an hour each.

1. **Fill in `media-masters/intake.md`.** Legal name, address, phone, email, hours, owner, services you actually sell, past clients who will give reviews, logo and brand assets, any existing accounts. The agents read this file.
2. **Lock the domain.** Preferred: `mediamastersgroup.com`. Fallbacks: `mediamastersgroupllc.com`, `mediamasters.agency`, `mmgdelmarva.com`. Buy the .com even if you brand around a shorter one. Check availability at your registrar; this environment could not run a WHOIS lookup.
3. **Trademark check.** A search surfaced a registered mark "MM Media Masters" for advertising services (USPTO serial 76406284). Have a trademark attorney confirm there is no conflict before spending on signage, ads, or a logo.
4. **Create and verify the Google Business Profile** at your real service address (or as a service-area business if you work from home). Pick primary category "Marketing agency," add "Website designer," "Internet marketing service," "Advertising agency." Add photos, services with prices, hours, and a booking link. Verification by postcard or video can take 1–2 weeks, so start now.
5. **Claim the rest of the map stack:** Bing Places (feeds Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, and ChatGPT search), Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps and Siri), Yelp, Facebook page, LinkedIn company page, Nextdoor business page.
6. **Set up measurement:** Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Bing Webmaster Tools, a call-tracking number (CallRail or similar), and a rank tracker that can compare you against d3corp.com (Semrush, Ahrefs, or BrightLocal's cheaper local tracker).
7. **Decide the content ownership rule:** who signs off on copy, who answers leads, and the promised response time (we recommend "every inquiry answered by a human the same business day").
8. **Unblock the agents' network access.** In this cloud environment, the network policy denied `d3corp.com`, so Phase 1 cannot run here until the environment's Network access is widened or these hosts are added under Allowed domains: `d3corp.com`, `www.d3corp.com`, `web.archive.org`, `pagespeed.googleapis.com`, `www.googleapis.com`, `validator.schema.org`, `fonts.googleapis.com`, `yelp.com`, `www.google.com`, `www.bing.com`. Steps: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cloud-environments#network-access. Alternatively run Phase 1 from Claude Code on a laptop, where there is no such policy.

## 5. Phase 2 — Build "everything they do, but better" (weeks 2–6)

### 5.1 Site architecture

Every D3 service gets a page. Then we add the services and pages they lack.

**Match (D3 has these):** Websites · SEO · Digital advertising (Google, Meta, Microsoft) · Social
media management · Email marketing · Graphic design and branding · Print · Video, photo, and drone
· Our work / case studies · Testimonials · About / team · Contact · Blog · Careers.

**Exceed (D3 lacks or hides these):**
- **Pricing page** with published starting-at prices and three packages per service.
- **AI Search Optimization** as a named service (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Copilot).
- **Public relations** (they mention it, they have no page).
- **Website hosting, care, and maintenance plans** with uptime and response commitments.
- **Reputation and review management.**
- **Hospitality and vacation-rental marketing** vertical page (Ocean City's biggest industry).
- **Restaurant and bar marketing**, **real estate marketing**, **medical practice marketing**, **home services marketing** vertical pages.
- **Location pages:** Ocean City, West Ocean City, Berlin and Ocean Pines, Salisbury, Pocomoke and Snow Hill, Fenwick Island and Bethany Beach, Rehoboth and Lewes, Millsboro and Sussex County, Easton and Cambridge.
- **Free instant website and SEO audit tool** (a lead magnet and a link magnet).
- **Transparent client reporting** page showing exactly what clients receive every month.
- **A working "scoreboard"** of our own site's speed and accessibility scores, live.

### 5.2 What "better" means, concretely

| Dimension | D3 today (per recon) | Media Masters Group standard |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | None public; SEO/PPC are add-ons | Published starting-at prices; no long-term contracts |
| Response | Form, phone, after-hours emergency email | Same-business-day human reply, live calendar booking, SMS, chat |
| Proof | Case studies, testimonial pages | Case studies with numbers, live Google review feed, before/after speed scores |
| Speed | WordPress on WP Engine (inferred) | Static, pre-rendered site; Lighthouse 95+ on mobile; Core Web Vitals green |
| Accessibility | Unknown; agents will score it | WCAG 2.2 AA, audited and stated on the site |
| Local reach | Ocean City and Salisbury | Nine location pages across Worcester, Wicomico, and Sussex counties |
| Content | Sparse blog | Weekly local-business marketing content plus free tools |
| Lead magnet | None seen | Free instant audit tool |
| Ownership | 40-person agency | You work directly with the owner; named account lead on every page |
| Reporting | Not shown | Monthly plain-English report, sample published on the site |

### 5.3 Technical choices

- **Stack:** Astro (static site generator) with plain HTML/CSS output, hosted on Cloudflare Pages (free, global CDN, custom domain, forms via a Worker). This repo's current site is plain HTML on GitHub Pages; that also works and the agents can build either. Avoid WordPress for the agency's own site: it is what D3 uses, it is slower, and it needs patching.
- **Forms and booking:** Cloudflare Worker or Formspree for forms; Cal.com or Calendly for booking; CallRail for call tracking.
- **Schema markup on every page:** `LocalBusiness`/`ProfessionalService` with both `sameAs` links and service area, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Review`/`AggregateRating` where allowed, `Person` for the owner, `Article` for posts.
- **Performance budget:** under 100 KB of CSS and JS per page, images in AVIF/WebP, fonts self-hosted, no third-party scripts above the fold.
- **New repository:** `media-masters-site`, separate from this invoice tool. This repo holds only the plan and agent definitions.

### 5.4 Content standards

- Every page: one target keyword cluster, a town or service in the H1 where natural, a first-screen call to action, a local proof block, an FAQ with 4–6 real questions, and internal links to three related pages.
- Case studies follow one format: client, problem, what we did, result in numbers, quote, screenshot.
- Author bylines with a real bio and photo on every article (E-E-A-T signals that AI search and Google both use).
- No stock-photo heroes. Real photos of the team, the offices, and Delmarva.

### 5.5 Build order and QA gates

1. Home, Contact, Pricing, Websites, SEO, About (the money pages) — week 2–3.
2. Remaining service pages and the first three case studies — week 3–4.
3. Location pages and vertical pages — week 4–5.
4. Audit tool, blog launch with 6 articles, reporting page — week 5–6.

No page ships without: Lighthouse 90+ mobile on all four scores, valid schema (validator.schema.org), zero broken links, spelling and reading-level check, and one human read-through. The `/build-mmg-site` skill drives agents through the blueprint and runs these gates.

## 6. Phase 3 — Be found more than D3 (week 4 onward, ongoing)

### 6.1 Technical SEO (one-time, then quarterly)
XML sitemap and robots submitted to Google and Bing; clean URL structure (`/services/web-design/`, `/locations/ocean-city-md/`); canonical tags; hreflang not needed; 404 and redirect map; image alt text; structured data as above; an `llms.txt` file for AI crawlers; IndexNow for instant Bing indexing.

### 6.2 Local SEO (the fastest win)
- **Google Business Profile:** complete every field, add services with prices, post weekly, answer every review within 24 hours, upload 10+ photos a month, use the Q&A section, add booking link, track calls and direction requests.
- **Reviews:** a review-request process (SMS plus email link after each milestone), a target of two new Google reviews a week, and never any incentives (Google bans them). Also route a few reviews to Yelp, Facebook, and Clutch.
- **Citations:** 50 consistent listings in month one (data aggregators plus the top directories), then local ones: Ocean City Chamber, Salisbury Area Chamber, Greater Salisbury Committee, Ocean Pines Chamber, Bethany-Fenwick Chamber, Rehoboth-Dewey Chamber, Maryland Office of Tourism services directory (D3 is in it), Worcester and Wicomico economic development pages, BBB, Alignable.
- **Second office signal:** D3 ranks in two map packs because it has two addresses. When budget allows, a Salisbury coworking address with signage and staffed hours is a legitimate second location.

### 6.3 Content and keyword plan
- The keyword sheet from Phase 1 maps every term to a page. Priority clusters: "web design + town," "marketing agency + town," "SEO company + town," "social media management + town," and the hospitality and restaurant verticals that are unique to Ocean City.
- **Weekly article** aimed at Delmarva business owners (seasonal marketing for Ocean City, how to get found before Memorial Day, Google Business Profile for restaurants, vacation-rental direct bookings, and the like).
- **Monthly free resource** (checklist, template, calculator) that earns links.
- **Comparison and alternative pages**, written fairly: "How to choose a marketing agency on the Eastern Shore," "Questions to ask before hiring a web designer in Ocean City." No disparagement; just the criteria we win on.

### 6.4 Authority and links
Local sponsorships (OC Air Show, Springfest and Sunfest, White Marlin Open, Salisbury festivals, youth sports), chamber memberships, Salisbury University and Wor-Wic business programs, guest columns in Coast Dispatch, Ocean City Today, Bayside Gazette, and Salisbury Independent, podcast appearances, free workshops for the SBDC, and the audit tool as a linkable asset. Target 10 new referring domains a month, all real.

### 6.5 Beyond Google
Bing Places and Bing Webmaster Tools (Bing powers Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, and ChatGPT's web results), Apple Business Connect, Yelp, Nextdoor, Facebook, LinkedIn, Clutch, DesignRush, UpCity, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, and Semrush Agency Partners (D3 is listed; we should be, with a better profile).

### 6.6 AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Copilot)
AI assistants recommend agencies by reading the same sources: directories, reviews, chambers, news, and clear entity facts. We publish consistent facts everywhere (`sameAs` schema, identical NAP), write FAQ-style answers to "best marketing agency in Ocean City MD" and its variants, keep Bing indexed (ChatGPT), and test the assistants monthly with 20 prompts to record whether we or D3 are named.

### 6.7 Paid bridge (weeks 3 onward)
Google Ads search campaigns for the money terms in Worcester, Wicomico, and Sussex counties, Microsoft Ads for the same at lower cost, and Meta retargeting. Suggested start: $750–1,500 per month. Pause terms as organic rankings arrive.

### 6.8 Reputation and brand search
Encourage branded search ("Media Masters Group Ocean City") through vehicle wraps, signage, event presence, and email signatures. Branded search volume is a ranking signal and the cheapest one to grow.

## 9. Risks and honest caveats

- **Domain age and links.** D3 has a 30-year head start. Nothing in this plan shortcuts that; it compounds by being consistent for a year.
- **Reviews need clients.** 25 reviews in 90 days assumes you have past and current clients willing to post. If not, the review target slips and paid search carries more weight.
- **Google's rules.** No bought reviews, no fake addresses, no keyword-stuffed business names, no private link networks. Any of these can erase a year of work overnight. Everything in this plan is white-hat.
- **The name.** "Media Masters" is used by others, including a registered trademark for advertising services. Get legal confirmation before investing.
- **Two offices beat one** in the map pack. Without a Salisbury address we can still rank there, but D3 keeps an edge in Salisbury until we have one.
- **Agents need network access.** This cloud environment blocked d3corp.com; Phase 1 runs locally or after the environment's allowed domains are updated.
- **The agents analyze; people decide.** Every claim in the agents' outputs is cited so you can check it. Pricing, positioning, and what you promise clients are your calls.

## Appendix B — Ethics line

We analyze what D3 publishes publicly, the same way any competitor or customer can. We do not
scrape private data, impersonate them, post fake reviews, buy links, copy their copy or designs,
or disparage them by name on our site. We win by being better and more visible.
