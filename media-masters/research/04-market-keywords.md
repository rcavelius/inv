# 04 - Market and keyword research: Delmarva digital marketing

Prepared for Media Masters Group LLC (Ocean City, MD). Research dated 2026-10-14; retries, verification and synthesis run 2026-11-11. Companion file: `keywords.csv` (301 keywords; 296 original plus 5 golf rows added 2026-11-11).

Every search volume in this document is an **estimate** with its basis stated. No keyword-tool data was used. Rankings come from the WebSearch tool, which **approximates Google**; they are not live Google positions.

---

## Summary

1. **D3 Corp has no town landing pages.** On its strongest searches, D3 ranks with its site map, homepage or raw `?p=` URLs (for example, #1 for "digital marketing agency Ocean City MD" with `d3corp.com/site-map`). Pages built for each town and service should be able to outrank those generic URLs.
2. **Salisbury SEO and Google Ads are the clearest openings.** For "SEO company Salisbury MD" and "Google Ads management Salisbury MD", D3 is absent and no locally based agency has its own page in the top 10. Only templated national pages rank (EZ Marketing, Prager, OneLittleWeb), plus pages for other Salisburys (UK, MA, PA).
3. **Build a Salisbury office page first.** Salisbury is the largest market (head term "web design salisbury md" is an estimated 50-200/mo). D3 does not appear for "web design Salisbury MD" or "website design Salisbury Maryland".
4. **Delaware beach towns are uncontested by D3.** D3 was absent from every Rehoboth, Lewes, Bethany, Millsboro and Sussex County snapshot. Those results go to directories and templated pages from Prager, EZ Marketing and AutomateNexus.
5. **Directory listings work as SEO.** Semrush Agency Partners, DesignRush, TechBehemoths, BBB, Clutch and INSIDEA-style listicles take most top-10 slots. Claim free profiles in the first month, and the Semrush partner listing once there is a Semrush subscription.
6. **D3 wins its verticals with named-client case studies, not service pages.** It is #1 for hotel marketing and #1-#4 for restaurant marketing in Ocean City. To compete, publish hospitality and restaurant service pages backed by named local case studies as soon as there are clients to name.
7. **Vacation rental marketing has no agency ranking.** AirDNA counts 7,166 active STR listings in Ocean City (Aug 2026), but only property managers and one national vendor (Bluetent) rank. A page aimed at OC property managers and brokerages is open.
8. **Publish prices.** D3 publishes none, and neither does any Salisbury agency found. Local pricing pages ("how much does a website cost in Maryland", "SEO pricing Ocean City") face almost no local competition.
9. **AI search and Google Business Profile services have no Shore provider.** Maryland AI/GEO providers are in Baltimore and DC. Claim "AI search optimization" and "Google Business Profile optimization" pages for Ocean City and Salisbury now, while volume is near zero and competition is nil.
10. **D3's weak points are trust signals and technical SEO.** No independent client review rating was found for D3, it shows no Google Partner or HubSpot badge, and duplicate legacy URLs are indexed. Build Google and Clutch reviews from the first client onward. Present Media Masters as the transparent, local, review-backed alternative.

## Method and limits

- **Tool.** All observations came from the WebSearch tool, which approximates Google. Positions are the order of results the tool returned. It does **not** show the Google map pack, People Also Ask boxes or Google AI Overviews. In this file, "AI summary: not observable" means unknown, not confirmed absent. WebFetch, curl and direct HTTP were blocked.
- **Volumes.** Every volume is an estimate in bands: very low (<10/mo), low (10-50/mo), medium (50-200/mo), high (200+/mo). Estimates are based on town population, how many competitor and directory pages target a term, BBB category sizes and comparable terms. BBB counts are directory listing totals, not demand. "Near me" and national question terms are national volumes; the Delmarva share is inferred from population.
- **Two dates.** Snapshots are labeled 2026-10-14 as instructed. The searches behind them ran on 2026-11-11, the environment date. Treat each snapshot as a single reading taken around those dates.
- **Search budget.** The session had a shared limit of 200 WebSearch calls. The keyword and SERP research used it up. The following retries were attempted on 2026-11-11 and **returned no data** because the budget was already exhausted:
  - Competitor profiles for Standout Fast, Full City Media, Sure Sites, Ocean Computer Tech, Studio For Publications, Prager Microsystems, EZ Marketing and MGH. Only D3 was profiled from live searches.
  - Most of the local authority source checks (cost, D3 presence). Those rows rely on the d3corp.com crawl, the 2026-10-13 recon and background knowledge, and are labeled that way.
  - All 10 SERP consistency re-checks (see below).
- **Name collisions** (Berlin, Germany and Berlin, CT; Lewes, UK; Cambridge, UK and MA; Salisbury, NC, PA, MA, MO and UK) pollute several result pages. Location pages need state names and local NAP (name, address, phone).

## SERP consistency check

- **Result: 0% agreement over the planned re-checks.** The data lists 10 keywords: 5 re-checks were attempted and 5 were not attempted. **This 0% does not mean the results changed.** None of the re-checks ran: the 5 attempted searches were refused because the shared 200-call budget was used up, and the other 5 were skipped for the same reason. The `d3_present_now = false` values in the data are placeholders, not observations.
- **What it means for confidence.** No SERP was observed twice, so movement over time could not be measured. Treat every position in this file as one reading. Positions within the top 10 are directional. The broad patterns are stronger, because they repeat across many independent queries: D3 has no town pages, directories dominate, templated national pages lead, and D3 is absent in Delaware.
- **Recommendation.** Re-run these 10 keywords in a new session with a fresh budget, and preferably in a rank tracker that reports real Google positions and the map pack, before committing large content budgets.

## Seasonality for Ocean City and Salisbury marketing buyers

**No search-trend or monthly volume data was collected, so everything in this section is inference** from the evidence listed with each point.

- **Ocean City's business base is seasonal** (data: about 7k year-round residents but a "large seasonal business base"; Bethany cited at about 950 year-round vs about 15,000 in summer; 7,166 active STR listings in OC per AirDNA, Aug 2026). *Inference:* hospitality, rental and restaurant owners plan and buy websites, branding and campaigns before the season. That points to **January to April** as the main buying window for new sites and redesigns, with launches before Memorial Day.
- **Shoulder seasons are a live marketing topic** (data: WMDT, WHYY and CBS Baltimore coverage of OC "second season" and shoulder-season events and business challenges; keyword "how to market a seasonal business in the off season"). *Inference:* September to November is a second window for off-season and shoulder-season campaigns (events, email to past guests, geofencing) and for planning next year's budget. Off-season articles are worth publishing in late summer.
- **Tourism budgets and awards cluster in the fall** (data: D3's Maryland Travel & Tourism Summit awards in Nov 2025; Worcester County tourism shifting its $587K marketing budget toward digital; town tourism ad contract news). *Inference:* public-sector and destination contracts are decided around fiscal-year planning. Monitor RFPs and the Town of OC and county tourism news year-round.
- **Rental owners act at season-end** (data: STR supply down 21.3% year over year; property managers' owner-recruitment pages rank). *Inference:* owners who are reviewing management or direct-booking options after a season are a fall/winter audience for "vacation rental marketing" content.
- **Salisbury is less seasonal** (data: regional hub with a year-round economy of medical (169 physician offices, TidalHealth), home services, university and professional firms). *Inference:* Salisbury demand should be steadier through the year, which makes it a good counterweight to Ocean City's seasonal cash flow.
