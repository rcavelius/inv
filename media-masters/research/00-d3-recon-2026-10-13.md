## Openings we observed (to verify in Phase 1)

1. No public pricing; SEO and PPC are contract add-ons. Price transparency is an open lane.
2. PR is promised on the homepage but has no service page.
3. Blog appears thin and dated for a 40-person agency.
4. Name and NAP (name, address, phone) are inconsistent across directories: D3 / D3Corp / D3 Corp / D-3 Corporation. Weak citation hygiene.
5. Review volume is modest for 30 years in business, and Yelp is nearly empty.
6. Employer reviews are middling (3.0 to 3.4), which can mean turnover; a "you always talk to the owner" positioning could land.
7. Geographic focus is Ocean City and Salisbury only. No visible location pages for Berlin, Ocean Pines, the Delaware beaches, or Sussex County DE.
8. Typical project budget under $10k (WP Engine listing) suggests mid-market pricing; room above and below.
9. They already market AI / generative engine optimization. We must match this, not skip it.
