A custom AI Assistant can help to bridge that gap and retain your visitors. This tool quickly answers questions, provides an overview of your services, and helps to convert those visitors into customers, even while you’re off the clock. Why Websites Need an AI Assistant 24/7 Availability: Your Website Never Sleeps Never miss a potential customer again. The AI Assistant can handle your most common and complex questions at any hour, so that your customers get immediate answers and your team can be free from constant monitoring. Customers get their questions answered, and those questions can be turned into leads by gaining valuable data that you can use to follow up. Easy Integration: Works on Any Platform No matter where your website lives, D3 can set up an AI Assistant for your company without any technical overhaul. Simply answer a quick questionnaire, and your AI Assistant will train directly on the information that’s on your site, and any third-party data source, such as your booking engine, pertinent white papers, etc. Customizable: Tailored to Your Brand Voice You can control the way the AI Assistant answers questions, what information is included, and the style or tone of voice. So, the conversation sounds like your brand and represents it accurately and confidently. Data-Driven Insights: Understand Your Visitors Better The AI Assistant will give you a recap of the types of questions it’s receiving and the conversation that it’s having with your customers, so that you can adapt your messaging accordingly. You’ll know exactly what visitors are asking and can identify gaps and understand what customers care about most. The perfect tie-in when implementing and perfecting your site for Answer Engine Optimization. How an AI Assistant Works on Your Website Because the AI Assistant is compatible with any website platform, the setup is simple. All you need to do is hone your responses to reflect your brand voice and offer answers to your most asked questions. As your AI Assistant is working to assist your customers, our D3 team will work with you on a follow-up process and on how to use the information gained through the AI Assistant. Additionally, you’ll start to see where clients get stuck in their purchasing process, to help make your sales funnel more efficient and client-centered. Quick Steps to Add an AI Assistant to a Website 1. Work with the D3 team to add the AI Assistant to your site. 2. Customize responses, branding, and tone. 3. Watch it greet visitors, offer helpful questions, and capture new leads automatically. As a conversation progresses, the AI Assistant will ask for contact details (typically a name and an email or phone number). These are securely passed along to your team for follow-up or to arrange a consultation if needed. Ready to join the future?
