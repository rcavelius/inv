It is your one-stop shop to update your business information, including your hours and contact info, while also allowing you to add photos, videos, status updates, share to circles, read and respond to reviews, and participate in Google Hangouts. Google My Business is one of the most important review sites since it is the first rating people see when they Google your business. Claim your business so you have complete control over the lisitng. D3Corp will help you in claiming your business so you maintain your online presence. Business owners can respond to the reviews left on their Google My Business page, giving them the last word. Google My Business also puts you on the map, literally. When someone does a Google search for your industry, your business will show up at the top of the search results with a red pin on Google Maps, so they know exactly where to find you. For example, if someone does a search for a crab house in Ocean City, MD, all local crab houses will show up as red pins in Google Maps, but the more optimized your Google My Business page is, the higher you will rank. D3Corp will optimize your page for you and link it to your website, making it easier for Google to crawl and determine the legitimacy of your business based on the search term. Linking the two together allows both your Google My Business listing and your site to rank higher in search results, which is the ultimate goal.
